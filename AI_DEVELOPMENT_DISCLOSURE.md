# AI-Assisted Development Disclosure

This prototype was developed with **AI-assisted coding in Cursor**. The clinical
QA problem framing, monthly LINAC workflow design, test definitions and
tolerances, film-analysis measurement intent, TRS-398-oriented dosimetry
workflow, acceptance logic direction, and packaging/deployment requirements were
defined by the author based on clinical medical physics practice. AI assistance
was used to accelerate software implementation. The author reviewed, tested, and
iterated on the system as a research/engineering prototype.

## Clarifications

- The author owns the clinical/scientific requirements and validation direction.
- AI assistance accelerated Django application wiring, helpers, documentation,
  and repository preparation.
- This is **not** a claim that every line was handwritten without assistance.
- Image analysis in this prototype is classical geometric / intensity
  processing, not a trained deep-learning QA model.
- The software remains a **research/educational prototype** and is **not
  intended for clinical use**.
