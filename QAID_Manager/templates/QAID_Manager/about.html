About QAID Manager

Research / educational prototype — not a medical device, not clinically validated, and must not be used for clinical QA release decisions.

Version {{ app_version|default:"1.4.2" }}

1. Application Overview

QAID Manager is a research/educational web-based Quality Assurance (QA) management prototype for Linear Accelerators (LINACs) used in radiation oncology education and workflow design. It explores how to streamline QA documentation, film-based geometric analysis helpers, and TRS-398-oriented dose calculation worksheets. It is not clinically approved software.

QAID Manager was developed to address the need for:

  • Centralized storage and tracking of QA data
  • Automated film analysis and dose calculation
  • Standardized reporting and documentation
  • Efficient scheduling and assignment of QA tasks

2. Purpose & Scope

QAID Manager is designed to support the full monthly QA process for LINACs. Its key functions include:

Monthly QA Workflows and Analysis

The system manages monthly QA schedules by assigning performers, tracking completion status, and handling extra or ad-hoc QA sessions. It automatically calculates due dates, takes holidays into account, and provides calendar-based views to simplify planning.

Managing and Reporting QA Data

QAID Manager organizes all QA test results, including:

  • Mechanical tests (field size, isocenter accuracy, table movements)
  • Beam quality tests (absolute dose, energy checks, symmetry, flatness)
  • Film-based measurements and analysis

The system can generate professional QA reports in DOCX/PDF format using customizable templates. The Statistics module provides department-wide trend review, LINAC comparison, and CSV export for ongoing QA oversight.

Compliance Tracking

Users can follow the status of each QA task (scheduled, in progress, completed, passed, failed, or requiring service). The system also maintains audit trails and supports workflow management for tests that do not meet acceptance criteria, including documentation and approval steps.

3. Key Features

  • QA scheduling with monthly calendar view and draft resume workflow
  • Task assignment for up to two performers
  • Guided film analysis wizard (field size, collimator, gantry) on the QA Entry form
  • Automated dose calculation for dosimetric QA (TRS-398 based)
  • Statistics module for trend review, LINAC comparison, and CSV export
  • Equipment service report tracking, filtering, and periodic printing
  • Status tracking with pass/fail indicators and failed-QA acceptance workflow
  • Report generation using user-defined DOCX templates (QA and service reports)
  • Database management for LINACs, dosimeters, physics parameters, QA tests, devices, and user accounts

For step-by-step instructions, open Help from the home page or navigation bar.

4. Intended Users

Medical Physicists

The primary users who perform QA tests, analyze results, and ensure compliance with regulations. They use the system for dose calculations, film analysis, and generating reports.

Dosimetrists

Support personnel who assist with QA measurements, film handling, and data entry. They may also help manage schedules and record results.

QA/QC Staff

Users responsible for overseeing quality assurance activities, monitoring QA progress, and reviewing documentation. They use the system to track compliance, manage schedules, and evaluate failed tests.

5. Acknowledgements / References

The QA procedures, tolerances, and workflows implemented in QAID Manager follow established international guidelines, including:

  • AAPM TG-142: Quality Assurance of Medical Accelerators
  • IAEA TRS-398: Absorbed Dose Determination in External Beam Radiotherapy
  • QCVN 13:2017/BKHCN: Vietnamese National Technical Regulation on Radiation Safety in Medical Facilities

Dose calculation algorithms are based on the IAEA TRS-398 protocol for absolute dosimetry, applying correction factors for temperature, pressure, polarity, and ion recombination.
